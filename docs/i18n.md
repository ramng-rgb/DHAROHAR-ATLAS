# Trilingual Internationalization (i18n) Architecture

Dharohar Atlas implements native trilingual support across **English (en)**, **Hindi (hi)**, and **Gujarati (gu)** to authentically represent regional heritage monuments from Gujarat and Rajasthan.

---

## 1. Architecture & Storage

- **Data Tier (PostgreSQL):**
  - Table `sites` provides native columns: `name` (English), `name_hi` (Hindi in Devanagari script), and `name_gu` (Gujarati script).
  - Trigram indexes (`gin_trgm_ops`) and future full-text search tsvectors index both Romanized and Indic scripts.
- **Frontend Presentation Tier (`apps/web/lib/i18n.ts`):**
  - Strongly typed translation dictionary supporting instant, zero-reload language switching (`EN` | `HI` | `GU`).
  - Typography tuned with Google Fonts `Noto Sans Devanagari` and `Noto Sans Gujarati` to eliminate glyph collision and preserve diacritic rendering.

## 3. Native Scholar Review Protocol

Machine-assisted and preliminary curatorial terms are flagged with `needs_native_review` in curatorial notes. Before the public archival release:
1. Indic epigraphy specialists will review Devanagari Sanskrit loanwords.
2. Regional historians will audit Gujarati dialectal variations for Saurashtra and Kutch vernacular architectural designations.
